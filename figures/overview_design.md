MiUrchin overview illustration
=============================

AI-assisted illustration generated with imagegen, using the MiCucumber project overview as the visual reference. Watercolor marine banner, six illustrative echinoid morphologies, four color-coded reference windows, and current primer-set names. Animal drawings are schematic rather than diagnostic species portraits.

The lower diagram is explicitly not to scale. Coordinates refer to extracted ungapped 16S rRNA of NC_001453.1, not the complete mitogenome. Numeric source: overview_regions.csv, transcribed from the reference seed windows in region_coordinates.csv with current S/M/L/XL labels. These seed windows are not fixed product lengths for optimized primers.

Current names: S, SA, SB, SD; M, MA, MB, MC, MD; L, LA, LB; XL, XLA, XLB.
